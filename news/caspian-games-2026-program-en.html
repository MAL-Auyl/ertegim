Caspian Games 2026: Competition Program Announced

📅 October 5–9, 2026 • 📍 Aktau

The program of the VIII International Student Caspian Games 2026 has been approved. Find out when and where each competition takes place and come support your favorite team!

Over five days, students from universities of Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Iran, Russia and Turkmenistan will compete in 10 sports at several venues across Aktau. Most competitions start at 10:00.

🎉 Ceremonies:

  • Opening Ceremony: October 5, 14:00 — Yessenov Sports Complex, 32nd Micr.
  • Closing Ceremony: October 9, 17:00 — Yessenov Stadium, 32nd Micr.

🏆 Competition Schedule:

🏊 Swimming October 5, 10:00 Yessenov Sports Complex, 32nd Micr.
🥊 Boxing October 5–8, 10:00 Khalyk Arena, 17th Micr.
🏀 Basketball October 5–6, 15:00 Yessenov Sports Complex, 32nd Micr.
🏐 Volleyball October 6–7, 10:00 Mangystau Arena, 33rd Micr.
🏃 Athletics October 6–7, 10:00 Yessenov Stadium, 32nd Micr.
⚽ Futsal October 6–7, 10:00 BS Arena, 40th Micr.
♟️ Chess October 6, 10:00 Yessenov Atrium, 32nd Micr.
🎮 E-Sports October 6, 10:00 Yessenov Technopark, 32nd Micr.
🥋 Karate-Do October 8, 10:00 Volna Sports Complex, 5th Micr.
🤼 Sambo October 8, 10:00 Caspian Sports Complex, 4a Micr., 47
