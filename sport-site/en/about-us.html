Educational programme

6B01401 – Physical Education and Sport

The educational programme is aimed at preparing specialists with pedagogical, coaching, organizational, and research competencies required for professional activities in the field of physical education and sport.

📘

What you study

Theory and methodology of physical education, sports games and athletics, gymnastics, swimming, health-enhancing physical education, the fundamentals of sports training, and methods of organizing sporting events.

🏫

Internships

Theoretical knowledge is combined with practical skills. Students undertake pedagogical and professional internships in secondary schools.

🤝

Dual education

Students enhance their professional skills through dual education and pedagogical internships at schools and specialized centers in the region.

Cooperation and quality

Partnerships and quality management

The Department places particular emphasis on developing professional cooperation with domestic and international higher education institutions, educational organizations, and sports institutions. Activities are carried out in the areas of academic mobility, professional exchange, research, and joint sports projects.

Yessenov University has implemented the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System, which is aimed at continuously improving the quality of education, organizing the educational process in accordance with modern requirements, and enhancing the professional training of students.

The Department of Physical Education and Sport is an academic community that brings together education and sport, science and practice, with a strong focus on promoting a healthy generation and preparing highly qualified professionals.

Join us on the path to sporting excellence, professional growth, and new achievements!
