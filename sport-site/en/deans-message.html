Dear applicants, students and colleagues!

Welcome to the official page of the Faculty of Philology and Sports! Our Department of Physical Education and Sport prepares competitive and professionally competent teachers, coaches and specialists in physical education and sport.

Our main goal is to bring together education and sport, science and practice. Our students combine theoretical knowledge with teaching internships in schools, training in sports clubs, and competing at regional, national and international events.

I invite you to join our faculty and take the path to sporting excellence, professional growth and new achievements with us!

Education. Sport. Health. Professionalism.

— N. Aitbayeva, Dean of the Faculty of Philology and Sports
