Goals and objectives

Department of Physical Education and Sport · Faculty of Philology and Sports

Goal

Our main goal

To prepare competitive specialists with the pedagogical, coaching, organizational and research competencies required for professional activities in physical education and sport.

Objectives

To achieve this goal

1Deliver the educational programme 6B01401 – Physical Education and Sport in line with contemporary educational requirements.
2Teach the theory and methodology of physical education, sports games and athletics, gymnastics, swimming, health-enhancing physical education and the fundamentals of sports training.
3Combine theory with practice: pedagogical and professional internships in schools and dual education at specialized centers in the region.
4Support sports clubs and activities that improve students’ athletic performance and promote a healthy lifestyle.
5Enable lecturers and students to take part in university, regional, national and international competitions, conferences, seminars and master classes.
6Develop cooperation with domestic and international universities in academic mobility, professional exchange, research and joint sports projects.
7Continuously improve the quality of education under the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System.

Education. Sport. Health. Professionalism.
