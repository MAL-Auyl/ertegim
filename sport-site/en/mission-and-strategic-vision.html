Mission and strategic vision

Department of Physical Education and Sport · Faculty of Philology and Sports

Mission

To prepare competitive and professionally competent teachers, coaches and specialists in physical education and sport in accordance with contemporary educational requirements.

Strategic vision

To be an academic community that brings together education and sport, science and practice, focused on promoting a healthy generation and preparing highly qualified professionals.

Strategic directions

How we get there

🎓

Quality education

Modern pedagogical technologies and digital educational tools; continuous improvement of teaching under the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System.

🏫

Practice-oriented learning

Pedagogical and professional internships in schools and dual education at specialized centers in the region.

🏅

Student sport

Improving students’ athletic performance and promoting a healthy lifestyle through sports clubs and activities.

🌐

Research and cooperation

Research, academic mobility, professional exchange and joint sports projects with domestic and international universities.
